EXCLUSIVE TO FALCON YOUNG

THE COMPANY

With strong global backing and a clear strategic plan, now is the very best time to join this international company in electrical automation, interface electronics and electrical interconnections. With a passionate attitude towards product leadership, marketing and client engagement, their values are based around creating partnerships with customers, fostering internal entrepreneurship and a devotion to creating innovative solutions through new technology. Having created this exciting role, due to huge potential upside, they currently require an experienced Channel & Product Manager to take on further development nationally in the electrical installation technology space.

THE ROLE

This challenge will see you working at Corporate Head Office level with Electrical Wholesalers, Global & Online Distribution and Retail Channels. Engagement with Category, Procurement, Central IT and Marketing within your client will enable you to deliver exceptional marketing campaigns, while ensuring the business has the right trade agreements to deliver outstanding results. Daily you’ll be working with the internal marketing team to develop new programs to drive growth through in-store merchandising and collateral.  

WHAT IS REQUIRED

The right professional will be able to display a history of success in dealing with wholesalers at a head office level and achieving growth through marketing initiatives and smart contract management. You will be seeking a role that requires you to articulate an attractive value proposition and a company that can deliver this. Your established skills and an earnest desire to win will enable you to develop, guide and close business by securing new retail channels, so thinking outside the box is essential.

WHAT IS ON OFFER

On offer, is the opportunity to work in a fun, engaging team environment, further develop the reputation of a globally recognised brand and become part of exciting growth. The package will consist of an attractive Base Salary, Car Allowance, Fuel Card, tools of trade and an above industry standard incentive.
