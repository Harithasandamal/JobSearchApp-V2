We are seeking a passionate and proactive Employment Specialist to join our Dandenong team

About Ostara Australia

Ostara Australia is a not-for-profit provider of the Inclusive Employment Australia (IEA) provider specialising in Mental Health commencing 01 November 2025.

Although this may be a new program – we have been transforming lives since 2001

With a people-first, individualised approach, Ostara empowers job seekers to find and maintain meaningful, long-term employment that aligns with their strengths, goals, and personal circumstances.

We are committed to breaking down the stigma surrounding mental health, and even within our own business we foster an inclusive and supportive environment where lived experience is not only embraced but seen as a valuable asset.

With a dedicated team and a values-driven culture, Ostara continues to make a positive impact in the lives of individuals by championing mental health advocacy and creating opportunities for sustainable and suitable employment..

 Key Responsibilities 

Working within the program guidelines, providing individualised support, and client-centred assistance and guidance to job seekers addressing their barriers to employment.

Reverse marketing our jobseekers to employers and maintain relationships with them by job carving, networking and understanding the local labour market.

Work with our jobseekers to ensure that they gain suitable employment opportunities aligned to their individual goals.

Support post-employment support to both employers and our clients.

Support the Business Manager (BM) to ensure the successful delivery of employment services by monitoring and overseeing the efficient and effective day to day operations of employment services, employees and resources at a site as required.

 Our Top 10

Demonstrated experience in a similar role within disability employment or a related employment support program, with a strong understanding of the employment services landscape and client-focused delivery.

Genuine passion for mental health advocacy, with valued lived experience and a commitment to empowering individuals through meaningful employment opportunities and social inclusion.

 Proven ability to manage and coordinate daily operations at the site, ensuring efficient workflow and client engagement, skilled in building rapport and conducting assessments of clients’ skills, barriers, and employment potential.

 Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive approach to identifying challenges and implementing effective, client-centred solutions.

Excellent time management, organisational skills, and proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, Outlook, and database systems.

 Demonstrated ability to motivate, encourage, and advocate for clients, fostering confidence and resilience in their employment journey.

Exceptional customer service skills, with a commitment to delivering a high-quality, responsive experience for clients and stakeholders with confidence to work on community partnerships and employer engagement.

Proven ability to manage competing priorities and multitask effectively in a dynamic and high-demand work environment, with high level of attention to detail, particularly in notes, reporting, and compliance.

Excellent verbal, written, and communication skills, with the ability to adapt communication style to suit diverse audiences.

Employment Services

Ostara Australia is a national not-for-profit organisation that works closely with the Australian Government, member organisations, communities and employers to place people disadvantaged through age, health, disability or cultural background into sustainable employment.

Our holistic approach is to address both the vocational and non-vocational barriers faced by disadvantaged job seekers. As such, we deliver personalised employment services, whilst also working in collaboration with countless community and welfare organisations to help job seekers with any mental, cultural or emotional challenges.

We are the largest mental health specialist within the Disability Employment Services sector, and are proud to have matched over 2000 employers with job seekers over the past year – creating more diverse and inclusive workplaces. Australia's largest disability employment service specialising in finding jobs for people with mental health problems or facing other disadvantage. Through advocacy we challenge the prejudice still unfairly associated with people disadvantaged through health, age, disability or cultural background, to create a more inclusive Australia. We achieve greater social and economic inclusion by finding sustainable employment for disadvantaged Australians through strong partnerships with employers, Governments and community welfare organisations. We credit becoming the largest disability employment service provider specialising in mental health to our holistic, personalised approach and our national network specialising in vocational and non-vocational services.
