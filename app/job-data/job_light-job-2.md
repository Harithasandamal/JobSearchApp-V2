Scheduling Coordinator

Do you love coordinating a workforce?

Be part of a fun and enthusiastic team

Career progression and training opportunities
 

Linc Height Safety Systems is a family-owned business, we focus on providing the highest quality service and supplying scaffolding solutions that are designed to meet our customer's specific needs, Servicing the housing construction industry within the greater Melbourne, Bellarine Peninsula and South East Queensland.

You will be joining our progressive and supportive team, that thrives on working together to ensure the best possible outcomes achieved for the business and our customers.

We are looking for an energetic, highly organised, customer focused individual to take on the position of Scheduling Coordinator. 

This position is critical to our success and as such it is a fulltime permanent position.

 

Duties of the role include (but certainly are not limited to):

Receiving and sending emails to be actioned daily 

Scheduling for trade and transport crews

Coordinating field employees and sub-contractors

Troubleshooting issues

Liaising with builders and trades

Maintaining accurate and up to date information in our system

Job costing analysis & weekly reporting 

Accurate data entry

Answering phone & general office duties
 

Ideal candidate attributes:

Resilient and thrives in pressure situations.

Is a team player, uses initiative to assist other staff members.

Excellent rapport building skills - internally and externally.

Process driven and always looking for opportunities to improve.

Reliable, effective and efficient in their work.

Highly organised with the ability to prioritise tasks.

Experience in construction / logistics and demonstrated customer service excellence.

High level of computer literacy.

 

If you have experience in coordination roles and possess exceptional communication, time management, customer service and organisation skills you are encouraged to apply. Candidates with relevant residential construction industry experience will have a distinct advantage.

 

If the above sounds like you and you would like to work for a company who will value your contribution, do not delay, ‘apply now’ or email ryan@lincscaffolding.com.au
