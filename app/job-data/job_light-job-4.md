Provide clinical leadership and expertise at an advanced nursing practice level, supporting nursing workforce development and growth through the coordination, development and delivery of best practice.

The Role

An opportunity has become available for a Nurse Educator to join Gold Coast Health (GCH) where the focus of our 12 000-strong team is on providing world-class health care to people living in the Gold Coast, and northern New South Wales region of Australia. 

You will

Utilise expert educational knowledge to lead the development, review and maintenance of education tools and resources relevant to care of older people and sub-acute nursing
Lead and develop the implementation, and review of pathways within the department to support orientation, onboarding and clinical progression of novices and experienced staff
Provide expert consultation to support organisational strategic direction
Work collaboratively with the Clinical Facilitators within the Surgical Services division to maintain a quality service
Provide clinical leadership within the acute units including support in times of staffing and clinical need 

About you

To be successful in this position you will require:

Bachelor of Nursing or equivalent  
A master's or higher qualification withing a relevant area is highly desirable.
Registration with the Nursing Midwifery Board of Australia
Evidence of vaccination, or be able to be vaccinated against and remain vaccinated against - Measles, Mumps, Rubella, Varicella, Pertussis and Hepatitis B
Tuberculosis screening, to identify if the tuberculosis vaccination may be required
Declaration of serious discipline history must be disclosed
Criminal History Screening will be required for new employees (GCH will cover cost)

About Us

The Nursing and Midwifery Education and Research Unit (NMERU) drives the development of an appropriately skilled nursing and midwifery workforce to deliver evidence-based, person-focused care to our community. The unit is led by the Director of Nursing NMERU, and sits within the Division of Nursing, Midwifery and Patient Experience.

 About Gold Coast Health

Nationally and internationally recognised location for innovative medical research and health care
Annual operating budget exceeding $1.5 billion
More than 1100 beds across three hospitals - Gold Coast University Hospital, Robina Hospital, Varsity Lakes Hospital and Robina and Southport Health Precincts
Secondary and tertiary health services across more than 20 facilities
