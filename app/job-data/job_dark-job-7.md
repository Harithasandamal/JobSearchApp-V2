The Company

Our client is a leading manufacturer of heavy-duty truck and bus bullbars. They specialise in high-strength, FUPS-compliant aluminium bullbars designed for safety, durability and performance.  With over 30 years of industry expertise, they offer in-house design, manufacturing and fitting services for brands such as Kenworth, Volvo, Western Star and many more. 

Responsibilities and duties will include, but are not limited to:

Surface Preparation: clean and prepare raw aluminium surfaces by removing scratches, weld marks and other imperfections using sanding tools and abrasives
Polishing: polish bull bars and aluminium components with both hand tools and polishing machines
Finishing: Apply final buffing and protective coatings as needed, ensuring a consistent & professional finish that meets stringent quality standards
Inspection: Perform thorough visual and tactile quality checks throughout the polishing process to guarantee all products are free from defects or blemishes
To be considered for this role you should possess the following:

Trade qualification in metal finishing (Cert III/IV) or equivalent hands-on experience in metal polishing or metalwork
Proven experience with aluminium or metal polishing using tools like grinders, buffers, and hand polishers
Strong attention to detail and ability to deliver high-quality, mirror-like finishes
Basic knowledge of workplace health and safety when working with machinery and chemicals
Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment, with good communication skills
Ability to communicate with fabrication, assembly, and quality assurance teams to ensure alignment on job
requirements and deadlines
