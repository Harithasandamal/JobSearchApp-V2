Customer Service Administrator
Location: Dandenong South, VIC 3175
Hours: Monday – Friday | 8:00 AM – 3:30 PM

We are seeking an enthusiastic and motivated Customer Service Administrator to join a highly respected, industry-leading company specialising in hardware systems, components, and software for the window and door industry across Australia and New Zealand.

This is a fantastic opportunity for someone with strong customer service skills who enjoys working in a fast-paced, supportive team environment.

About the Role: Key Responsibilities:


Provide prompt and reliable support to customers and the external sales team via phone and email.
Process, monitor, and manage customer orders and enquiries.
Liaise with the warehouse team to ensure accurate and timely dispatch and delivery of orders.
Offer technical assistance to customers and sales staff.
Maintain accurate records of all customer interactions and communications.
Support warehouse operations as needed, including:

Order picking and packing
Assisting with customer deliveries
Receipting of incoming stock


What You Need:
Strong customer service focus with effective problem-solving skills
Excellent verbal and written communication skills
A confident and professional phone manner
Experience in the window and door industry (highly desirable)
Proficiency in Microsoft Office, especially Outlook, Word, and Excel
Experience with Pronto CRM or similar systems (desirable)
Valid Drivers Licence


What You Get:
Permanent, full-time role
Attractive salary package
Immediate start available
Supportive and friendly work environment
Opportunity to work with a well-established industry leader
How to Apply:
Click the “APPLY NOW” button to submit your updated resume (preferably in an easily readable Word format). Please ensure your contact details, including mobile number and email address, are correct so we can get in touch.
