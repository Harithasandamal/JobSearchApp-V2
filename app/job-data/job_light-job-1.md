About Us
Brown & Watson International (BWI), part of the Amotiv LP&E division, is home to powerhouse brands such as NARVA, Projecta, OSRAM, and Philips Automotive. With a focus on innovation and a strong commitment to our people, we’re proud to lead in the automotive electrical and portable power space.

The Role
We're seeking a highly organised and proactive Group Project Coordinator to join our Growth team. Reporting into Program Management, you'll play a key role in enabling delivery of strategic workstreams and new product development across cross-functional and international teams.

Key Responsibilities

Coordinate project documentation, timelines, and reporting

Facilitate team communication, meetings, and workshops

Analyse data, track metrics and support prioritisation

Support governance and adoption of project processes

Drive collaboration across Product Development, Manufacturing, Marketing, and Sales

What You Bring

Strong project coordination and communication skills

Experience in cross-functional environments (automotive preferred, not essential)

Commercial acumen and problem-solving ability

Self-motivated with a ‘can-do’ attitude and collaborative style

Relevant tertiary and project management qualifications (e.g. PMP)

Why BWI?
Be part of a values-driven company that defines innovation as "change that adds value." We offer a supportive team culture, career development opportunities, and the chance to help shape industry-leading products that power professionals and enthusiasts alike.
