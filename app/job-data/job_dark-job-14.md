About the role

Coolman is seeking a Refrigeration Maintenance person to join our team on a casual basis. This dynamic role is based in Upwey VIC 3158 and will involve providing expert maintenance and repair services for a variety of commercial refrigeration systems around Melbourne. Check our website www.coolme.com.au to see what we do. 

What you'll be doing

Conducting regular maintenance checks on refrigeration equipment. 

Maintaining detailed records of all work performed

Providing exceptional customer service and building strong client relationships

What we're looking for

Ability to work alone at various sites around melbourne. All training will be provided. 

Strong technical skills and problem-solving ability

Ability to use power tools and hand tools

Excellent communication and customer service skills

Ability to work independently and as part of a team

A valid driver's licence and reliable transportation 

Valid Working with children or willing to apply

Valid ASIC or willing to apply. To work in Melbourne airport. 

What we offer

The right applicant will become full time employee with company car. You will start from site every morning. 

About us

Coolman is a leading provider of commercial refrigeration solutions. With over 20 years of experience in the industry, we are dedicated to delivering exceptional service and innovative products to our valued clients. Our company is built on a foundation of quality, reliability, and a commitment to environmental sustainability.

Apply now for this exciting opportunity to join the Coolman team!
