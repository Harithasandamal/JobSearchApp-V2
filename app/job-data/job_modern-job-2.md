About our Organisation 

As Australia's largest timber door manufacturer, Corinthian Doors has taken its famous brand around the world, securing an international reputation for quality, innovation and design. 

About our Opportunity 

We have an opportunity for an experienced Afternoon Shift Warehouse Supervisor responsible for the supervision of our PM warehouse operations including receiving, storage, picking customer orders, loading and transporting of our bulk customised freight as well as inventory control. This is a critical role for our Victorian operations requiring someone to lead from the front ensuring we effectively service and deliver to our customers. As the Supervisor for the PM shift, you will lead and support our warehouse workforce of up to 20 personnel as well as oversee a small production team of 8 with responsibilities of closing the manufacturing site at the end of the shift. 

In addition, you’ll:

ensure compliance and continual improvement in all aspects of safety including conducting toolbox talks and safety observations regularly
lead, coach and support warehouse staff to ensure high performance, engagement, and adherence to operational standards.
oversee daily warehouse activities including receiving, put-away, picking, packing and dispatch to meet DIFOT targets.
maintain accurate stock levels through cycle counts and support stocktake activities. Investigate and resolve inventory discrepancies.

Permanent full-time role, hours are:

Monday to Thursday 2:30pm - 11:30pm and Friday 1:30pm - 10:30pm

About You

We’re searching for someone who has a background in warehouse operations preferably in the building material manufacturing industry or with bulk customized freight in a team leadership role. We’re looking for a strong people leader who has a flexible leadership style and hands on approach, who can maintain high level of employee engagement with the ability to adapt their communication style to the needs of their diverse team and customers.

In addition:

experience with ERP / WMS Systems
ability to analyze data and reporting
working knowledge of Chain of Responsibility
Forklift Licence (LF) essential
hold a current unrestricted driver’s license.
